# Play Store Listing Copy

## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
Plan your week, focus with a timer, and study offline. No account needed.
```
(74 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
StudySphere turns a deadline into a plan you can actually follow.

Tell it what you're working toward and when it's due, and Autopilot builds
your week — one confident action, not another form to fill out. Every
study day is broken into focus blocks sized to how much energy you have,
so a heavy week doesn't mean an unrealistic one.

Works completely offline. Everything you create — tasks, plans, notes,
flashcards, exam sprints — stays on your phone. There's no account to
create, no password to remember, and nothing is ever sent to a server.

What's inside:

Autopilot — describe your goal, deadline, and available time, and get a
full week of focus blocks, sequenced from understanding a topic through
practicing it to recalling it under pressure.

Tasks — capture what you need to do, tag it with a subject and priority,
and get a real reminder when it's due.

Focus timer — a Pomodoro timer that keeps accurate time even if you switch
apps or lock your phone, because it's built on the clock, not a fragile
countdown.

Notes & Summarizer — paste your raw notes and get a summary, key terms, a
revision checklist, and quiz questions, generated instantly on your device.

Flashcards — spaced review that gets a card out of your way for longer
each time you know it, and back sooner when you don't.

Exam Mode — tell it how many days are left and how confident you feel, and
get a revision sprint sized to match.

Sage — a study guide that looks at your real tasks and exams, not generic
advice, and tells you what to do next.

Your data is yours. Export everything to a file whenever you want — that's
how you move to a new phone. Delete everything permanently, any time, from
Settings.

Built for students who need a plan more than they need another app to
manage.
```
(1,533 characters — well under the 4000 limit; room to expand once real
screenshots/testimonials exist, deliberately left tight rather than padded).

## Notes on wording

- No "#1", no competitor names, no "AI" (Sage and the Summarizer are
  deterministic and on-device — see docs/AUDIT.md — calling them "AI"
  would be a false claim, not just a style choice).
- "Works completely offline" leads the full description on purpose — it's
  the single most defensible, checkable claim StudySphere can make, and
  the audience (metered data, mid-range phones) cares about it more than
  a feature list.
